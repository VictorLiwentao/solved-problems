# Equal power-sum coordinates beyond symmetric functions

Wentao Li · Research draft prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

## Original problem

Does agreement of the type I and type II quasisymmetric power-sum coordinates characterize symmetric functions? [Jean-Christophe Aval, Karimatou Djenabou and Peter R. W. McNamara, 2022](https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.273) (Section 5.2).

## Result

No. On two-part compositions of each weight, agreement imposes a single linear equation. In every degree at least five this permits nonsymmetric functions.

The computation uses the two standard power-sum bases of Ballantine, Daugherty, Hicks, Mason and Niese. [Basis formulas](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.11613)

**Significance 43/100.** An explicit counterexample and an unbounded family answer the general converse. They follow from the published change-of-basis formulas. Independent review of the proof and priority is pending.

## Original problem

Does equality of the composition invariant ζ imply equivalence by switching? [José Aliste-Prieto, Jeremy L. Martin, Jennifer D. Wagner and José Zamora, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10333) (Section 7.3, Question 1).

## Result

Equality of ζ is equivalent to equality of substring-composition multisets of the binary cut words. Two compositions of 24 have equal ζ and different coarsening polynomials.

The binary words are due to Acharya, Das, Milenkovic, Orlitsky and Pan. The result identifies what their construction implies for the later switching question. [Original string construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.2439)

**Significance 60/100.** Characterizes equality of the invariant by a string reconstruction criterion and disproves its proposed converse to switching. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

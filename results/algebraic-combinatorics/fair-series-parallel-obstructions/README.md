# Forbidden subposets for fair series-parallel orders

## Original problem

Which forbidden subposets characterize fair series-parallel edge-decorated posets? [Doriann Albertin, Jean-Christophe Aval and Hugo Mlodecki, 2024](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/90/ajc_v90_p341.pdf) (Remark 4.2).

## Result

The underlying order must avoid an induced N. No vertex can have upper covers of both colors, or lower covers of both colors. These conditions are sufficient.

The tests use underlying induced subposets and decorated convex subposets. The source leaves the minor operation unspecified. The proof also gives a convex-only formulation and explains why a minor relation allowing arbitrary cover deletion cannot work. [Earlier containment conventions](https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.273)

**Significance 50/100.** Gives a finite recognition criterion for the whole recursive class. It extends a forbidden-pattern characterization for the smaller class generated by singleton ordinal sums. Independent review of the proof and priority is pending.

## Reproducible check

The recursive definition and the forbidden-pattern test agree on all 5,449 decorated orders enumerated through five vertices. [Verification script](verify.py)

# The point-mass conjecture for deep linear spectral equilibrium

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

The point mass is the unique minimizer and the only law satisfying the equilibrium force equation. For quadratic loss, the excess energy bounds the mean squared distance from that point.

**Original question.** Does the deterministic continuum equilibrium have a spread of singular values or consist of a single point mass? [Chen, Kotwal and Menon, 2025, Section 8.3, equations (8.4) to (8.6)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06137)

**Scope and prior work.** The normalization follows the original integral equation. The finite-width stochastic fluctuation problem uses a different functional.

**Significance estimate.** 60/100. It resolves the stated continuum conjecture for all finite depths and all the proposed Schatten exponents. The finite-width minimizer and the entropy concavity theorem were already known.

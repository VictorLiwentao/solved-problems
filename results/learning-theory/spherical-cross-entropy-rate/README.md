# The best finite-class rate for spherical cross-entropy

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

The negative logarithm of the optimal loss gap has order N to the power 1/s times log N on a sphere of intrinsic dimension s. The same rate corrects the pair-energy bound printed in Theorem 5 of arXiv v2.

**Original question.** How rapidly can finitely many class directions approach the continuum value of tied spherical cross-entropy? [Lu and Steinerberger, 2020, Section 1.3, question before Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.08465v2)

**Scope and prior work.** Lu and Steinerberger already proved uniformity of the continuum minimizer. The proof uses the optimal spherical design existence theorem of Bondarenko, Radchenko and Viazovska. It concerns fixed positive inverse temperature and logarithmic order, with dimension-dependent constants.

**Significance estimate.** 69/100. It gives matching upper and lower rate scales, including a lower bound for the nonlinear loss itself. The model ties the classifier weights to the features.

**Review status.** Written argument. Independent proof and priority review are pending.

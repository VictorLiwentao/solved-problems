# A semicircle profile for the cold deep linear singular-value gas

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

At every finite depth, each continuum equilibrium concentrates at the predicted point. The rescaled end-to-end singular values have a semicircle law with an explicit depth-dependent variance.

**Original question.** What are the location, width and shape of a continuum singular-value gas as its noise tends to zero? [garry-gum, following Menon, 2026, Problem 2(b), finite-depth concentration and profile](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/32)

**Scope and prior work.** Menon proposed studying the large-width equilibrium. The cited August 2026 formulation specifies a continuum gas and asks for its cold profile. Infinite depth, the finite-width large-deviation principle and fixed-temperature edge universality remain open here.

**Significance estimate.** 63/100. It identifies a full fluctuation profile for this continuum model, beyond concentration alone. Microscopic finite-matrix statistics require a different limit.

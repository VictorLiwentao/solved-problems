# Simultaneous noisy mistake and last-mistake bounds for finite language classes

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

One generator retains the finite-class noisy mistake bound at every horizon and has a bounded time of last mistake for every unknown finite noise budget.

**Original question.** Jon Kleinberg, Charlotte Peale and Omer Reingold (2026), §§7.1 and 8, finite-class noisy guarantees. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10809)

**Significance estimate.** 66/100. The argument combines the published sharp-form mistake inequality with protection against arbitrarily late corrupt observations.

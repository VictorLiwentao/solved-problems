# The clustering threshold for sharply concentrated noisy attention

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

The threshold has the predicted logarithmic and log-log terms and the stated additive constant in every fixed dimension. Every nonuniform equilibrium minimizing the free energy at coexistence has a Gaussian profile after centering and rescaling.

**Original question.** What is the sharp large-inverse-temperature threshold for a discontinuous transition, and what shape do the concentrated minimizers have? [Mun and Rosenzweig, 2026, equations (1.18) and (1.19)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05140)

**Scope and prior work.** Mun and Rosenzweig prove the phase diagram and describe the asymptotic estimates as ongoing work. Balasubramanian, Banerjee and Rigollet analyze stationary branches and transitions on the circle. The theorem here concerns global minimizers.

**Significance estimate.** 68/100. It derives a global threshold and the shape selected at coexistence in a nonlinear model of attention. The original authors predicted the constant.

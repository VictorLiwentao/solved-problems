# The quadratic threshold gap at tricritical noisy attention

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

The critical couplings separate quadratically in every fixed dimension. A positive sixth-order coefficient determines the leading constant and forces the global minimizers onto the predicted local branch.

**Original question.** Does the gap between global coexistence and loss of linear stability grow quadratically on the discontinuous side of the tricritical point? [Mun and Rosenzweig, 2026, equation (1.17)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05140)

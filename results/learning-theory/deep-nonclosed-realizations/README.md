# Nonclosed ReLU realization spaces at arbitrary depth

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

At every hidden depth of at least two, one fixed architecture has a limit outside its realization space, both uniformly on a rectangle and in every finite-p first-order Sobolev norm.

**Original question.** Can the nonclosedness of scalar ReLU realization spaces persist in deeper fixed architectures? [Martinet and Bungert, 2026, Remark 3.11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09887)

**Scope and prior work.** The first widths are four and eight, and all subsequent hidden widths are one. The question of strong W¹,∞ closedness on a connected domain and the one-dimensional compact-interval question remain open here.

**Significance estimate.** 60/100. The same geometric obstruction survives every length of scalar tail. The earlier two-hidden-layer construction supplies the core example.

**Review status.** Written argument. Independent proof and priority review are pending.

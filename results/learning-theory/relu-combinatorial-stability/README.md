# Generic combinatorial stability of deep ReLU networks

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 21, 2026

At almost every choice of weights and biases, small perturbations preserve the entire labeled activation complex up to a continuously varying homeomorphism.

**Original question.** For a fixed biased ReLU architecture, is almost every parameter combinatorially stable, with a continuously varying homeomorphism of the entire input space that preserves the labeled cells? [J. Elisenda Grigsby, Kathryn Lindsey, Robert Meyerhoff and Chenxi Wu, 2022, Section 5.2 and Definition 5.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.04036)

**Significance estimate.** 70/100. Resolves a structural conjecture at every finite depth and width. The contribution is the application of compatible semialgebraic triviality to all activation signs, including boundary cells.

**Proof status.** Written argument awaiting independent review. **Priority status.** A targeted search through November 21, 2026 found the following related results. Independent priority review is pending.

Finkel, Fu and Wu proved a shallow bias-free characterization and generic pattern stability in the deep bias-free setting. They identified the passage to combinatorial stability as the remaining step in November 2026. The topological theorem used in the draft is due to Hardt.

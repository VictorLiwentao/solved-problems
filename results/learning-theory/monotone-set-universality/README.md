# Universal monotone set networks on infinite ground sets

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

Triangular-hat sums and monotone decoders uniformly approximate every continuous inclusion-monotone target on bounded-size multisets over a compact ground set. A single countable bank suffices and fixed finite banks cannot serve every target.

**Original question.** Does the finite-domain monotone universality theorem extend to infinite ground sets? [Sarangi, Sverdlov, Dym and De, 2025, Section 7 and Theorem 14 (NeurIPS 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23634)

**Scope and prior work.** The positive result allows width to increase with accuracy. The negative result applies to a fixed finite encoder for every target when cardinality at least two is allowed. On noncompact ground sets the statement uses compact-open approximation.

**Significance estimate.** 62/100. It resolves the continuous approximation question and gives an explicit width bound at fixed spatial resolution. It uses classical lattice approximation and the authors’ order-separation theorem.

**Review status.** Written argument. Independent proof and priority review are pending.

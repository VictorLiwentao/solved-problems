# Equal-length Tamari orbits with unequal down-degree totals

Wentao Li · Research draft prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

## Original problem

For a fixed boundary path, must equal-length rowmotion orbits in its Tamari lattice have the same total number of lower covers? [Colin Defant and James Lin, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.10464) (Conjecture 10.2).

## Result

Two ordinary Tamari orbits of length 22 have down-degree totals 45 and 46. The same obstruction holds in all alternate Tamari lattices for the same boundary.

Eu, Hioe and Lee proposed the later extension. The transfer uses the invariance theorem of Adenbaum and coauthors. [Extension, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29431) · [Transfer theorem, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12983)

**Significance 65/100.** Disproves a general conjecture about Tamari lattices and the homometry clause of a later extension. The witness verifies directly in the ordinary lattice and transfers to every increment vector for its path. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

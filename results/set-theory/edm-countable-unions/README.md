# The Erdős–Dushnik–Miller theorem and countable unions

**Wentao Li**, with assistance from **OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra**. [Paper (PDF)](edm-countable-unions.pdf) · [LaTeX source](edm-countable-unions.tex)

In ZF, selecting one point from each of countably many countably infinite sets and enumerating their union are different tasks. Howard and Tachtsis proved that two forms of the Erdős–Dushnik–Miller graph theorem suffice for the first task. Their Question 3 asks whether either suffices for the second. [Original paper, Lemma 5.6 and §8](https://doi.org/10.4064/fm250514-20-7)

**The draft gives a model of pure ZF in which both graph theorems hold and a countable union of countably infinite sets is uncountable.** The first uncountable ordinal is regular in this model. The stronger of the two graph theorems gives every uncountable graph a countably infinite independent set or an uncountable clique.

The proof starts with countably many rows of atoms. A support may contain finitely many entire rows and a set of density zero in each remaining row. This allows each row to be enumerated while preventing a single enumeration of their union. A small-index theorem for permutation groups reduces the graph argument to copies of individual rows. Infinite Ramsey theory then produces the required homogeneous set. The last section transfers the separation to pure ZF. [Dixon, Neumann and Thomas](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/18.6.580)

The negative implication is relative to the consistency of ZF. The manuscript gives a theoretical proof. Independent proof and priority review are pending. [Authors’ preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18229)

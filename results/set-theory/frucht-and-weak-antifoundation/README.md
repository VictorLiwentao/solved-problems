Over ZF without Foundation, Frucht's theorem is equivalent to all three weak representation principles CWFAFA₂, WFAFA₂ and WFAFA′₂.

**Original question.** Junhong Chen and Daheng Ju, 2026. [§§7–8, the three implications in the second open question](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23891v2)

## Argument

A single application of Frucht's theorem supplies a rigid graph containing a copy of the target vertex set. Finite expressions over that graph give distinct labels for every vertex that can arise later. These labels make Chen and Ju's graph expansion work without making a new choice at each stage.

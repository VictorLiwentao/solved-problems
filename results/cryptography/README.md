# Cryptography

[All drafts, A–Z](../README.md) · [Scores and original questions](../../docs/scores-and-sources.md#cryptography)

The protocol drafts cover private simultaneous messages, conditional disclosure of secrets, card-based secure computation and zero-knowledge proofs of proximity. The Boolean-function drafts study nonlinearity and cancellation on inputs of fixed Hamming weight.

For arbitrary finite alphabet size, a Latin square admits ideal PSM exactly when it is a relabelled group table. | 64 |

An entropy argument relates noise to communication along every unqualified path and applies to nonlinear messages as well. | 61 |

Over larger projective lines, the same construction realizes precisely the three-party predicates expressible as an XOR of pairwise terms, with one marked point per sender. | 53 |

For three or more single-bit senders, the only symmetric examples are parity, its complement and the two constants. | 67 |

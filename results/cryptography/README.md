# Cryptography

[All drafts, A–Z](../README.md) · [Scores and original questions](../../docs/scores-and-sources.md#cryptography)

The protocol drafts study what a one-bit evaluator can compute privately and what a verifier can learn by querying a large input. The Boolean-function drafts study nonlinearity and cancellation on inputs of fixed Hamming weight.

For three or more single-bit senders, the only symmetric examples are parity, its complement and the two constants. | 67 |

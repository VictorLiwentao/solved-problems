\begin{center}{\Large\bfseries A packing-preserving lift and uniform\\ Catalan representatives}\par
\small Wentao Li \enspace$\cdot$\enspace Prepared with assistance from OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra \enspace$\cdot$\enspace 18 November 2026\end{center}

Blitvi\'c, Kammoun and Steingr\'imsson ask for operations that preserve permutation-packing counts and suggest the existence of Catalan representatives for every overlap $m>2$ and length $K>2m$. We construct representatives throughout $K\ge 2m$. The same operation preserves the entire sequence of packing counts for a wider class of starting patterns. [\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29846v1\#S7}{Part I, \S7, item 6}]

\subsection*{A lift that preserves every count}
Fix a permutation $p$ of length $k$ and overlap $r$ with $1\le r\le k/2$. Write $p=PIS$, where $P$ and $S$ have length $r$. Assume $P,S$ have the same relative order and $1$ occurs in $P$. For $j\ge1$, place $j$ copies of $p$ on consecutive windows of stride $k-r$, requiring the total order of the positions in each window to agree with their ranks in $p$. The transitive closure of these orders is $Q_j(p,r)$. Its linear extensions correspond bijectively to the permutations with the prescribed occurrences, by assigning the values $1,2,\ldots$ in extension order. Other occurrences are allowed.

Choose $t\ge1$ and $h\ge0$. Write $B=2t+h$, and let primes denote the operation of adding $B$ to each letter. Define a permutation $p^*$ of length $k+2t+h$ by concatenating the following three words, respectively its prefix, middle and suffix:
\[
 \big[(t+h+1)\cdots(2t+h)\ P'\big]\quad
 \big[(t+1)\cdots(t+h)\ I'\big]\quad
 \big[1\cdots t\ S'\big].
\]
Here each displayed interval is increasing and one with length zero is empty. The new overlap is $r+t$. It is admissible because the $t$ new letters in each boundary word are smaller than all its old letters, with the remaining relative orders inherited from $P,S$.

\textbf{Theorem.} For all $j\ge1$, the lifted packing poset is the ordinal sum
\[
 Q_j(p^*,r+t)\ \cong\ C_{(j+1)t+jh}\ \oplus\ Q_j(p,r),
\]
where $C_s$ is an $s$-element total order and $A\oplus B$ places each element of $A$ below every element of $B$. In particular, the number of linear extensions is unchanged for every $j$.

\textit{Proof.} Number the windows $1,\ldots,j$. The new $t$-letter prefix block in window 1 is $D_0$. The corresponding suffix block in window $i$ is $D_i$, also the prefix block of window $i+1$ when $i<j$. Write $H_i$ for the $h$ new middle letters in that window. Boundary words are disjoint within a window since $2(r+t)\le k+2t+h$. Thus these are all the new positions and their only identifications. Window $i$ orders them as $D_i<H_i<D_{i-1}$, with each block internally ordered. For $h=0$ the comparison $D_i<D_{i-1}$ still holds. The new positions therefore form a chain with $(j+1)t+jh$ elements, ending at the greatest element of $D_0$.

Delete the new positions and retain all comparisons generated within each old window. This yields exactly the original identifications and the poset $Q_j(p,r)$. No additional old-to-old comparison can use a new position because every old letter in a window exceeds every new letter in it. Let $a_i$ be the old minimum of window $i$. For $i>1$ it belongs to that window's old prefix and hence also to the preceding window. Consequently $a_1\le\cdots\le a_j$, so $a_1$ lies below every old element. Window 1 puts all of $D_0$ below $a_1$. This proves the ordinal sum. The new elements have to occupy the first $(j+1)t+jh$ places in their unique order in every extension, giving the claimed bijection.\done

\subsection*{Catalan representatives for every requested length}
Take $p=1324$, $r=2$, $t=m-2$ and $h=K-2m$. For every $m\ge3$ and $K\ge2m$ this gives the prescribed length and overlap. For example, $m=3$ and $K=7$ give $3462157$. The seed has packing count $\frac1{j+1}\binom{2j}{j}$, as already recorded in Part I, Proposition 5.37. Here is a direct proof for clarity.

Name its successive two-position boundary blocks $(L_i,R_i)$ for $0\le i\le j$. Window $i$ imposes $L_{i-1}<L_i<R_{i-1}<R_i$. The global bottom and top are $L_0$ and $R_j$. Removing them leaves two increasing chains $L_1,\ldots,L_j$ and $R_0,\ldots,R_{j-1}$, with the remaining relations generated by $L_i<R_{i-1}$ for $1\le i\le j$. An extension interleaves these two chains so that in every prefix the number of $L$'s is at least the number of $R$'s. Such words are counted by the Catalan number. The theorem carries this enumeration to every representative.\done

\textbf{Related work and verification.} The original paper credits Liese and Remmel for the seed and gives a separate family of linked length and overlap, $K=2a$, $m=2a-2$. Nagy proved the double-factorial portion of item 6 using a forced chain and a comb. Our lift applies to arbitrary seeds meeting the stated hypotheses and gives the Catalan portion for independently specified $m,K$. Searches of the original paper, its companion and indexed literature through 18 November 2026 did not locate this lift or a previous uniform solution of the Catalan portion. The proof awaits independent review. [\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2610.29846v1\#S5}{Part I, \S5}, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06937}{Nagy}, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12829}{Part II}]

The accompanying \texttt{verify.py} independently builds the window posets on their actual positions and checks every order relation against the asserted ordinal sum. It makes 4,356 comparisons over admissible seeds of lengths two through six, four lifting parameter choices and three numbers of windows.

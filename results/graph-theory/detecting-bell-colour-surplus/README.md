# Can a Bell colouring graph detect a surplus colour?

Wentao Li · Research draft prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

[Proof (PDF)](detecting-bell-colour-surplus.pdf) · [LaTeX](detecting-bell-colour-surplus.tex) · [Citation](citation.bib) · [Exact check](verify.py)

## Original problem

Does the Bell k-colouring graph determine whether k equals the chromatic number of its underlying graph? [Brian Hearn, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13005) (Section 1, first question preceding Conjecture 1.6).

## Result

For every palette of size at least two, the same Bell graph can arise at the chromatic number and with a spare colour. The ambiguity can persist even when both graph order and palette are given.

Hearn reports earlier Bell-graph coincidences due to Rozhoň and Šámal. This construction compares an optimal palette with a surplus palette.

**Significance 41/100.** Answers an explicit recognition question with elementary witnesses, including a collision at identical palette and order. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

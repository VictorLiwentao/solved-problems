## Original problem

Does recording the size, boundary and number of leaf edges of every subtree distinguish trees? [José Aliste-Prieto, Jeremy L. Martin, Jennifer D. Wagner and José Zamora, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10333) (Section 7.5, Question 2).

## Result

Two 23-vertex trees agree on the counts of all 14,532 subtrees by all three statistics. Adding the same positive number of pendant leaves at each vertex gives an infinite family.

The authors give earlier examples for weaker invariants. Michael Tang’s example in their revised Section 7.2 separates the full and half-generalized degree polynomials. The result here concerns their separate Question 2.

**Significance 61/100.** An explicit obstruction to reconstruction persists under an arbitrary uniform attachment of leaves. It resolves a proposed refinement of two classical tree invariants. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

# Equal chromatic square counts for trees with different degrees

Wentao Li · Research draft prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

[Proof (PDF)](square-count-and-tree-degrees.pdf) · [LaTeX](square-count-and-tree-degrees.tex) · [Citation](citation.bib) · [Exact check](verify.py)

## Original problem

Does the square-count polynomial of a tree determine its chromatic pairs polynomial or its two-vertex generalized degree data? [Simon MacLean, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05807) (Section 5, implication diagram).

## Result

Two trees have identical induced-square counts for every number of colours, but one has 15 leaves and the other has 16. Both proposed implications fail.

MacLean supplied the formula expressing the square polynomial in terms of degrees and the degree sums on edges. The counterexample uses that formula as a coefficient identity.

**Significance 57/100.** Separates two graph-colouring invariants on the class of all trees and answers two implications in the source diagram with one witness. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

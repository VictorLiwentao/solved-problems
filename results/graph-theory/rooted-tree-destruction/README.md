## Original problem

Does the destruction polynomial determine a rooted tree? Does the distribution of the number of surviving vertices determine it? [Fabian Burghart, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08394) (Conjecture 18 in v1, Conjecture 20 and the following question in the published version).

## Result

Two nonisomorphic 39-vertex rooted trees have the same trivariate polynomial and the same distribution of the number of vertices left at separation. The construction gives arbitrarily large examples.

Burghart supplied the smaller pair with equal admissible-subtree polynomials and the probability integral. The new step rearranges four copies of that pair.

**Significance 63/100.** Resolves the principal outstanding invariant conjecture in the source. Its fringe-subtree argument then applies to asymptotically almost every rooted tree. Independent proof and priority review is pending.

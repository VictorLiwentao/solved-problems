# Ten questions about density, Borel rank and compactness

Wentao Li · November 22, 2026 · Research drafts prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex

Seven manuscripts give proposed answers to ten questions or specified parts of questions. Three answers use the same random-forcing model, and two use the same obstruction for summable ideals. Each row identifies the original formulation and the axioms used. Independent checking of the proofs and a full priority review are pending.

| # | Original question | Proposed answer | Significance |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | **Can an ideal characterize a circle subgroup complete for its maximal Borel class?** [Paolo Leonetti, 2026, Q6.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23801) | Yes in ZFC. One sequence of powers of two works for every Borel ideal, including at countably infinite ranks. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/universal-wadge-rank/README.md) | 71 |
| 2 | **Must the permutation cardinal for oscillating density be at least the covering number of null sets?** [Brech, Brendle and Telles, 2024, Q37](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21102) | It can be smaller. After adding ℵ₂ random reals to a GCH model the two values are ℵ₁ and ℵ₂. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/random-density/README.md) | 63 |
| 3 | **Does the same lower bound hold for oscillating relative density?** [David Valderrama, 2025, Q4.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21059) | It can fail in the same model. Every random name has a ground-model subsequence on which its density converges almost surely. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/random-density/README.md) | 60 |
| 4 | **Can the interior input densities always be changed without affecting the permutation cardinal?** [Brech, Brendle and Telles, 2024, Conjecture 41](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70300) | The conjecture as stated has a consistency counterexample. Adding density 1/2 to the sole input type “oscillation” raises the cardinal in the random model. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/random-density/README.md) | 58 |
| 5 | **Can all interior densities require a larger family than density 1/2?** [David Valderrama, 2025, Q4.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21059) | ZFC gives equality for both the reaping and permutation cardinals in the question. The proof extracts independent splits whose biases remain away from zero and one. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/interior-density/README.md) | 62 |
| 6 | **Is the van der Waerden ideal Katětov below the Ramsey ideal or the difference ideal?** [Filipów, Kowitz and Kwela, 2023, Q1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06881) · [Q7.8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06907) | Both comparisons fail in ZFC. Every labeling of the edges of an infinite clique admits an infinite subclique whose labels contain no genuine three-term arithmetic progression. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/ramsey-progressions/README.md) | 59 |
| 7 | **When does convergence on the whole circle force the support of the integer sequence into the ideal?** [Dikranjan, Das and Bose, 2020, §3](https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/fundamenta-mathematicae/all/249/2/113188/statistically-characterized-subgroups-of-the-circle) · [Filipów, Kwela, Leonetti and Tryba, 2026, Q2.19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12192) | Failure is equivalent to one Bohr ideal reducing to a positive restriction. A Haar-measure test proves the property for all nonpathological analytic P-ideals, including density zero. The original authors anticipated that particular case. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/bohr-support-test/README.md) | 53 |
| 8 | **Is the harmonic summable ideal Katětov-homogeneous?** [Filipów, Kowitz and Kwela, 2023, Q8.8(2)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06907) | No in ZFC. Every tall summable ideal has a positive restriction whose partial weight sums grow too slowly for the required reduction. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/summable-restrictions/README.md) | 53 |
| 9 | **Does the harmonic ideal's Bolzano–Weierstrass property imply the hereditary version?** [Filipów, Kowitz and Kwela, 2023, Q10.4(1a)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06907) | Under CH there is a compact separable Hausdorff counterexample. It follows from the obstruction in row 8 and the authors' comparison theorem. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/summable-restrictions/README.md) | 47 |
| 10 | **Is every differentially compact space hereditarily differentially compact?** [Filipów, Kowitz and Kwela, 2023, Q10.4(2a)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.06907) | Under CH there is a counterexample. In ZFC the hereditary property equals Ramsey compactness. Kowitz and Tryba had already identified the related isomorphism between restricted ideals. [Proof](../../../results/set-theory/hereditary-differences/README.md) | 48 |

## What the proofs add

The highest score goes to row 1 because one construction works at every Borel rank in the question. Earlier work encoded a single ideal membership test and established the sharp numerical rank. Encoding countably many tests gives the stronger continuous-reduction property that Leonetti asked about. The manuscript also proves a general upper bound for continuous functions on arbitrary Polish domains, covering the formulation in Kwela's introduction. [Leonetti, Theorems 2.2–2.3 and Corollary 3.14](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23801) · [Kwela, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03432)

Rows 2–4 all come from applying a classical subsequence theorem of Komlós to the Boolean values of a name in random forcing. CH gives equality in the two proposed lower bounds, while the random model refutes them. Row 6 also gives, under CH, a compact separable Hausdorff Ramsey space which fails van der Waerden compactness, answering the Ramsey clause of Question 16.6. The Hindman-ideal comparison and the two finite-product questions require different arguments. [Komlós, 1967](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02020976) · [Filipów, Kowitz and Kwela, §16](https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2024.8)

## Priority and verification

Leonetti already proved Polishability for analytic P-ideals in August 2026 and the matching upper Borel bound on the circle. Those earlier results receive no new-result score. Kowitz and Tryba answered Question 8.8(1) about the difference function. The summable question in row 8 is part (2). Jana Flašková's 2008 and 2014 talks ask the related finite-product question for harmonic ideal spaces. Row 9 concerns the hereditary property. [Leonetti, Theorems 2.2 and 2.11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23801) · [Kowitz and Tryba, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00153-025-00994-1) · [Flašková, 2008](https://home.zcu.cz/~blobner/research/Hejnice2008.pdf)

The finite scripts check the graph shapes in row 6 and the possible formal-root cancellations in row 10. The infinitary and forcing arguments are written proofs. The manuscripts have no Lean formalizations.

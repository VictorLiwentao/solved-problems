# Training dynamics of deep linear networks

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 21, 2026

These ten manuscripts study what gradient flow preserves, which completion a partially observed network selects, and how training behaves when its weights grow without bound. Six give proposed answers to specific published or publicly posted questions. Four prove additional results about broader questions. All links below lead to the arguments and the original sources. Proof and priority await independent review.

The scores estimate mathematical significance conditional on correctness and novelty, with the Riemann hypothesis and three-dimensional Navier–Stokes at 100. They refer to the result in the manuscript. None of these ten arguments has a Lean formalization.

## Which results depend on which?

The proof of proper escape constructs an ordinary Euclidean gradient flow in factor space. The depth-two paper extends this construction across a loss of one rank and uses it to exclude convergence to saddles for almost every initialization. The directional paper applies a recent theorem about the total angular motion of definable gradient trajectories to the same lift. The deterministic point-mass conjecture and the semicircle law concern two different energies. The semicircle law takes the continuum limit as given, then lets the noise decrease to zero.

## Literature checks

- The ICLR 2026 paper by Marcotte, Peyré and Gribonval already derives the infinite-depth training metric under balanced neural-ODE initialization and proves completeness for deep ReLU path-lifting. Both are prior work. Its Section 3.1.1 explicitly leaves the overwide two-factor problem open. [ICLR paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07370) · [Peyré's November 2026 account](https://www.gpeyre.com/blog/2026/11/12/intrinsic-factor-training-dynamics/)
- Chen, Kotwal and Menon already find the deterministic optimizer at each finite width. They ask about other equilibria of the continuum equation. The point-mass manuscript proves uniqueness directly over probability measures and supplies a quadratic stability bound. [Source, Section 8.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06137)
- Treviño already establishes almost-everywhere unboundedness at depth greater than two. The questions about bounded returns, normalized directions, depth two and Gaussian end-to-end initialization are posed separately in Section 12. The general directional-convergence theorem used above is due to Do, Jentzen and Kuckuck. [Treviño](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05551) · [Do, Jentzen and Kuckuck](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29211)
- Menon's large-width question dates to 2024. The explicit low-noise gas and its proposed fluctuation scale were posted by garry-gum in August 2026. The finite-temperature many-particle law and the continuum variational problem have distinct orders of limits. [Menon, Section 14.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09004) · [Original formulation, Problem 2(b)](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/32)

The Gaussian completion law at higher depths, generic decay of the diagonal residual at finite temperature, a full classification of escape directions, and estimates uniform in both temperature and initialization remain open in these manuscripts.

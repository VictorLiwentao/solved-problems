# Ten questions in deep learning theory

Wentao Li · With assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

Five manuscripts answer specific questions about graph refinement, sparse networks, adversarial training, identifiability and a proposed equilibrium density. Five companion notes prove results under the hypotheses stated below and identify the broader questions that remain open. All ten arguments and their priority await independent review. Scores estimate mathematical significance on the archive’s scale, with the Riemann hypothesis and three-dimensional Navier–Stokes at 100.

## Priority checks

Kachaiev, Burger, Kabri, Korolev and Roith proved full support for anisotropic attention equilibrium measures on November 14, 2026. The Fourier manuscript above tests a separate approximation proposed in 2025. [Full-support theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19518)

Xia and Xu had already proved a finite-sample identification result for irreducible shallow networks of a given width. The manuscript on opposite pairs studies local identification from samples away from activation boundaries. [Finite Samples for Shallow Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12744)

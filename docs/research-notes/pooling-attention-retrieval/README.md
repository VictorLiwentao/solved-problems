# Research notes on ReLU training, set networks and attention

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 21, 2026

The main manuscript answers two questions from Yuandong Tian’s ICML 2017 paper on the population loss of two ReLU neurons. The second manuscript determines the limiting covariance rank in a Gaussian attention experiment posed by Castin and colleagues. The table records the precise disposition of ten questions. Scores are estimates of significance if the arguments are correct and new, with three-dimensional Navier–Stokes at 100. Independent proof and priority review are pending.

| Question and original source | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Does Tian’s interpolation residual detect which side of the student cone a teacher lies on? [Tian, 2017, Conjecture 1](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/tian17a.html) | Proposed proof for every opening of the two coplanar, noncollinear student rays. This supplies the missing hypothesis of Tian’s Theorem 4. **Interrelated ReLU results together 65/100.** [Proof](../../../results/learning-theory/tian-cone-sign/tian-cone-sign.pdf) |
| 2. Can a critical point have one teacher inside the cone and one outside? [Tian, 2017, immediately after Theorem 4](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/tian17a.html) | Yes. For every student opening there are linearly independent teachers giving noncollinear positive-loss strict saddles with exactly this membership. The analytic branch and its negative curvature appear in the same manuscript. [Proof and check](../../../results/learning-theory/tian-cone-sign/README.md) |
| 3. Which covariance directions survive random-value Gaussian attention? [Castin, Ablin, Carrillo and Peyré, 2025, Figures 4 and 11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18322) | Proposed resolution of the limiting-rank observation for both heads. Every bounded trajectory with a hyperbolic value matrix converges, with limiting image exactly the key kernel. **60/100.** [Proof](../../../results/learning-theory/gaussian-attention-limit-rank/README.md) |
| 4. How wide must finite-order Janossy pooling be to approximate every scalar set function? [Wagstaff et al., 2022, §6.1.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.01959) · [Syed, Nambiar and Siegel, 2026, §5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08377) | Matching upper and lower bounds of order $nd$ for every fixed interaction order and polynomially growing input dimension. The unrestricted joint dependence on order, size and dimension is open. **64/100 for the stated regime.** [Proof](pooling.md) |
| 5. Which sparse retrieval instances are hardest to embed with large margin? [Bangachev, Bresler, Kogan and Polyanskiy, 2026, Problem 1 and Appendix D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23556) | Among all uniform query families, a weighted block-design condition characterizes the worst unrestricted margin. The proof includes its exact value and the least dimension attaining it. The original problem also asks for the whole margin curve in restricted dimensions for arbitrary incidence matrices. **61/100 for the characterization.** [Proof](retrieval.md) |
| 6. Does the full Boolean leap exponent lower-bound Gaussian correlational query complexity? [Abbe, Boix Adserà and Misiakiewicz, 2023, after Proposition 5](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v195/abbe23a.html) | No. A bounded-query algorithm beats the exponent for every odd Hermite degree of at least five. Partial-trace estimators in later literature already supply the underlying mechanism. The online-SGD conjecture is separate. [Proof and prior art](gaussian-queries.md) |
| 7. Which distributions obey the Gaussian formula for ReLU population gating? [Tian, 2017, §7.1](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/tian17a.html) | The radius-squared-weighted angular measure must be uniform with total mass $d$. The two-neuron kernel determines that entire measure. Rotationally invariant sufficiency is known, and the converse uses classical cosine-transform uniqueness. [Derivation and examples](relu-input-laws.md) |
| 8. What embedding dimension permits every possible strict top-$k$ token set? [Basri and Jacobs, 2026, §5](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/d9b42c5f263a3f7486e50b84d6bbc0d3-Abstract-Conference.html) | Already determined by Martínez-Sandoval and Padrol. A query exposes precisely the selected $k$-sum among all sums of $k$ document vectors iff it ranks that subset strictly above its complement. Exchanging one member proves the reverse implication. Their Theorem 1.1 gives the dimension for every $n,k$. [Original geometry result, 2019 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.01189) |
| 9. Can one generic polynomial activation separate tuples of distinct polynomial inputs of every degree? [Kohn, Marchetti, Massarenti and Mella, 2026, Conjecture 1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27113) | Unresolved here. Chang and Hendi prove the bounded-input-degree statements. Their admissible Zariski-open set may depend on the degree bound. Intersecting countably many such sets does not establish the nonempty Zariski-open set required by the stronger statement. [Chang and Hendi, Theorem 1.1, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20825) |
| 10. Are all two-hidden-layer ReLU realization spaces uniformly closed on an interval? [Martinet and Bungert, 2026, Remark 3.10](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09887) | Unresolved here. A uniform procedure decides closedness for each supplied architecture and can return a witness when closedness fails. It gives no termination criterion for the search over all widths. [Reduction to real quantifier elimination](interval-closedness.md) |

The repository catalogue contains two manuscripts from this investigation. The Gaussian attention paper studies fixed continuous-depth heads with Gaussian inputs. The ReLU paper uses the exact population loss with two teachers and two students. Neither manuscript establishes a general convergence law for training deep networks.

# Equilibria and generic escape at finite-temperature depth two

Wentao Li · Prepared with assistance from OpenAI Codex · November 2026

All full-rank equilibria are classified in every width. A critical temperature removes the single-minus equilibria. Almost every trajectory escapes in norm even when corank-one limits are accessible.

**Original question.** What replaces the finite-temperature critical-point and escape theorems at depth two? [Treviño, 2026, Section 12.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05551)

**Scope and prior work.** The source proves the deeper-network theorem and identifies depth two as exceptional. The signed factor charts in the proof allow the stability argument to include corank one.

**Significance estimate.** 65/100. It gives both the temperature transition and an almost-everywhere dynamical theorem. A basin-by-basin classification of singular limits is still missing.
